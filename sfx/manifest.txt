# Gravity Hollow — SFX manifest (canonical)
# file | event id | description of the sound | usage context
# All clips: MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0, 48 kHz mono Opus 96 kbps, loudness-normalized (-20 LUFS), 100 inference steps.
# Event ids are the names passed to AudioEngine.event() in src/audio.js (SFX_SAMPLES) or theme beds in AMBIENCE_SAMPLES.

sfx/ui-move.opus | ui_move | A very short soft wooden tick, like a fingertip tapping a hollow gourd, dry and quiet, no reverb tail | Any focus/hover move in menus and the camera-reset tray button (main.js btn-camera)
sfx/ui-confirm.opus | ui_confirm | A short warm marimba pluck with a gentle rising pitch, rounded mallet attack, positive and clean | Every primary button press: Play, mode cards, Start, Resume, Retry, Next (main.js bindChrome, ui.js buildModeCards)
sfx/ui-back.opus | ui_back | A short muted woodblock knock falling slightly in pitch, soft and unhurried, single dry hit | Back buttons, Pause opened, Leave match (ui.js data-back, main.js pauseMatch/leaveMatch)
sfx/invalid-buzz.opus | invalid | A short low dull buzzer thunk, like a rubber mallet striking a padded metal door once, flat and discouraging | Rejected action: undo unavailable / nothing to undo (main.js doUndo)
sfx/eat-morsel.opus | eat | A small crisp crunch, like biting into a dry seed pod, with a faint bubbly gulp at the end | Player void consumes a crumb or chunk (rules step event eat, mass < 8)
sfx/eat-gem.opus | eat_gem | A bright crystalline chime, like a small glass bell tapped once, sparkling with a short shimmering decay | Player void consumes a gem; also each tutorial marker visited (main.js onEvents, TutorialRun.onFrame)
sfx/eat-void.opus | eat_void | A deep airy whoosh collapsing inward, like a heavy cloth sack being sucked shut, followed by a low resonant thud | Any void swallows a rival that is not the player (main.js onEvents eat_void)

sfx/goal-chime.opus | goal | Three ascending tuned hand-bell strikes, warm brass bells, celebratory but gentle, with natural ringing decay | An objective completes; also each tutorial step passed (main.js onEvents goal, TutorialRun.advance)
